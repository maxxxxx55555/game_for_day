# Country-data attribution — Heirs of Fortune 2.0.1

The bundled 192-row historical table in `scripts/BirthCountries.gd` identifies its source year as 2023 and its source as World Bank World Development Indicators. An open, unencrypted copy of the exact bundled numeric rows is supplied in [BIRTH_DATA.csv](BIRTH_DATA.csv).

| Input | Attribution and indicator |
|---|---|
| Population | United Nations Population/Statistics Divisions, national statistical offices and Eurostat; distributed by World Bank: [SP.POP.TOTL](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL). |
| Crude birth rate | United Nations Population/Statistics Divisions, national statistical offices and Eurostat; distributed by World Bank: [SP.DYN.CBRT.IN](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.CBRT.IN). |
| Life expectancy | United Nations Population Division, national statistical offices and Eurostat; distributed by World Bank: [SP.DYN.LE00.IN](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.IN). |
| GDP per capita | National statistical organizations/central banks, OECD and World Bank staff estimates; distributed by World Bank: [NY.GDP.PCAP.CD](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.PCAP.CD). |

Each linked indicator page identifies its license as [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The [World Bank dataset terms, including their additional terms](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/legal/terms-conditions/datasets), apply to reuse and onward sharing. Retain this attribution and these license/terms links with redistributions. Metadata and licenses were checked on 2026-10-28; later data revisions are not silently substituted into this frozen game table.

Adaptations: a selected country table; estimated annual birth weights derived from population and crude birth rate; rounded numeric values; ordering by birth weights; region codes and translated names. Starting cash, health, intellect, costs, income modifiers and the bounded character death age are fictional game-balance transformations. Country population life expectancy shown on the birth card is separate from the character death-age rule. The data providers do not endorse this game. This attribution concerns statistical inputs and does not grant rights to unrelated game art or music.
