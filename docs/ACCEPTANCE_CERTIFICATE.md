## Scope and limitations

The soak is a finite stability review at a 454,537,216 B allocation plateau: engine node and resource counts are exactly flat with zero orphans, and operating-system allocation variation is measured against a scaled allowance starting where the per-cycle engine counts stop changing, rather than described as literal zero-byte growth. Object counts in this build warm from 2023 to a 2109 plateau at 583.636 s because the appearance system caches both skins' surfaces; that bounded warm-up is measured as warm-up, and counts that never settle are a failure — 8 synthetic profiles are graded through the rule to show the leak cases still fail. The balance sweep runs two scripted policies over four birth origins and three seeds per variant, so it measures the shipped knobs and not every possible player policy. Old failed attempts remain evidence and are not counted as passes. Gallery endings and dynasty rows are explicitly labelled as driver-seeded. QA harnesses share the real user-data profile with the game, so save-writing harnesses and suites are never run concurrently. The paper skin keeps the dark boot splash, which is shown before any theme is applied. Finite automated checks do not replace a human playtest or listening review.

Portable EXEs are not Authenticode signed. Android/emulator and store certification are outside this Windows release. Offline integrity resists ordinary corruption and editing but cannot prevent an owner from patching a local binary. The custom native template necessarily contains the pack-decryption key; encryption is not invulnerable DRM. No secrets or private profiles are distributed as loose files.

After the gated release and the five owner checks in [HANDOFF](HANDOFF.md), this edition may be announced and distributed within these limits.
