# Heirs of Fortune 2.1.0 — final release report

Publication status: **2.1.0 is published and re-issued.** The publication gate returns PASS at the commit named in `docs/acceptance-2.1.0/gate.json` (engine 4.7.stable.official.5b4e0cb0f, Windows x64, clean tree); the previous re-issue gated at `b38e9df3788b8d83cc6c0a7956614963d6c16819` on 2026-10-31 and the tag `v2.1.0` names the current gated commit. The release carries exactly two assets, `HeirsOfFortune-Windows-2.1.0.zip` and `HeirsOfFortune-SHA256SUMS-2.1.0.txt`, whose digests and the fresh-download check are recorded at the end of this report and in the [acceptance certificate](ACCEPTANCE_CERTIFICATE.md).

The edition was first published on 2026-10-30 from `807ba09` (archive SHA256 `c7199222573d17c8996d8774ffdd6238feef240753eaf803b137eba38b456226`, EXE `000b0eda79d7fa463a1e0e9ff64ebcfd976ad11b7fddd07dcccc01319e0fead9`, PCK `35ed80d81ef2b22712115af74ca0b99702c037536812f809020242f453652ca1`). That build is superseded, not deleted: it stays reproducible from `807ba09`, and its binaries are kept on the build host. It was re-issued because `main` had in the meantime taken the two-skin appearance system, so the published build and the published source disagreed about what 2.1.0 is. The earlier published release [2.0.1](https://github.com/maxxxxx55555/game_for_day/releases/tag/v2.0.1) is untouched, and the 2.0.2 candidate was never tagged or published.

Automated acceptance at the gate: strict compile over 38 shipping scripts with all 49 warning categories as errors; 50 headless and 51 rendered source cases in Russian and in English with zero failures; four exports; twelve exported-binary runs (three victories, three defeats, two dynasty-ledger routes and four guard refusals); 100 of 100 seeded cycles with 100 distinct final states in 2,084.9 s; 1.4.0 and 2.0.x profile round-trips; a 1,807.078-second rendered soak with 60 complete cycles, 954 checkpoints, 1,806 samples and zero failures. Total gate wall time 5,148.6 seconds. Full receipts: [certificate](ACCEPTANCE_CERTIFICATE.md), [kit](ANNOUNCE_KIT.md), [release notes](RELEASE_2.1.0.md), machine-readable set in `acceptance-2.1.0/`.

Balance: 112 scripted lives in 1,540.9 seconds under a conservative and an aggressive policy over the four birth origins, with no dominance finding. Meteor reaches victory about three years earlier than its siblings and pays for it in measured stress; heir pays a tenth of quarterly income for a larger inheritance and finishes later; steady is the reference. Perks and ascension levels shift age and stress without producing a free win.

Performance: measured against a 2.0.2 worktree in interleaved attempts on this host with isolated save profiles — median frame 16.687 ms versus 16.675 ms, p95 17.226 ms versus 17.263 ms, dashboard ready 522 ms versus 545.5 ms against a baseline attempt spread of 159 ms. No regression is claimed beyond that noise, and no every-device claim is made.

Presentation: re-rendered at `2ff705d`, whose only difference from the exported commit is documentation — 136 gallery frames (dark and light skins, both locales, 540x960 and 720x1280) in eight labelled contact sheets, plus 24 ambition-row frames with each ambition selected in both skins. The anti-AI-slop audit passes over displayed copy, 26 documented colour tokens across both skins, all seven interface vectors at 1.75px, fonts and licences.

## Defects found and repaired while closing this edition

- QA capture folders were being packed into the shipped PCK; `export_presets.cfg` now excludes `builds/`, and the repository isolation check refuses a tracked path under it.
- The soak trend rule reported allocator oscillation at a few hundred megabytes of plateau as a leak; the allowance now scales to the plateau, and the post-warm-up window starts where the engine counts stop changing rather than at a fixed 120 s. Eight synthetic profiles are graded through the rule to prove it still fails 150 B/s, 1 KiB/s and ~6 MiB/run leaks, a never-settling object count and orphan nodes.
- The heir ambition was strictly dominant — upside with no cost. It now trades a tenth of quarterly income for its inheritance bonus, and both the model cost and the disclosure on the description the player reads are asserted by tests.
- Bulk document spacing had edited byte-pinned promise snapshots; the snapshots were restored verbatim and the promise inventory re-hashed, rather than loosening the promise test.
- The mechanics matrix carried an `execution_evidence` path that never existed in the repository; it now names the suites that actually run, and ambitions gained their own row with per-column assertions that exist.
- The appearance system's skin applier cleared every button font-size override, which widened the bottom bar past the 540 and 360 layouts and printed raw choice text over the choice cards; only stylebox overrides are cleared now.
- The dynasty ledger and the milestone card never repainted on a skin change, the appearance row in settings printed Russian item labels in the English build, and ledger and ambition copy used one hardcoded grey that vanished on the paper skin.
- The quote graph showed no unit, scale or axis until something resized it; the choice card measured its height against a width that no longer existed once the numeral gutter was added; the documented roman numerals were never drawn because no card received its index; the intro cover painted past its bounds over the caption; two new icons were cut at 1.5px instead of the documented 1.75px.
- A skin switch left everything that took a colour while its screen was built: the intro brand, the ambition hint and description, the outcome line, ledger rows and backdrop, currency and milestone surfaces kept the previous skin, while the outcome tones, the delta chips and the finale carried hardcoded hex copies of the dark tokens. Text colour is now named by token and repainted on switch, the literals are gone, and the finale is measured as an intentionally staged sheet rather than excused from the check.
- The credits overlay painted its backdrop once at build time and never listened for a skin change. Because the intro toggle is reachable by keyboard behind that overlay, switching skins with credits open left near-black body text on the previous dark sheet — a readability defect. The panel now repaints its own backdrop and the theme case drives exactly that path.
